Its relative luminance increases monotonically from approximately 0.0244 to 0.8971, and remains ordered under the included approximate protan/deutan/tritan transforms. ## Escape-time-aware reliability windows

The former fixed per-transition deadline (2 smoke / 4 paper slow-time units) could stop a trajectory before visible retrieval. The new policy is implemented in `experiments/run_correlation_phase_plane.py`:

1. At each gain, evaluate the original orthogonal initial-entry escape time and stable return-map escape time.
2. Choose the initial per-edge time budget as

   `max(configured_minimum, 3 * max(initial_escape, asymptotic_escape) + 1 + hold_time)`.

3. Run the same cycle-completion and escape-detection criteria as before. Both panels use the final trial and the same time convention, t/tau_slow.
4. Only for a timeout, restart from the **same seed**, extending the deadline to at least twice the preceding budget and at least `3 * max(predicted_escape, observed_escape, observed_switch) + 1 + hold_time`. This reproduces the original stochastic path up to its previous termination.
5. Stop after at most three retries or at 60 slow-time units per edge. Wrong-memory, collapse and nonfinite outcomes are not retried as successes. Remaining timeouts are explicitly right-censored observations at their recorded horizon; they are not claims of dynamical impossibility.

The global allowance is the per-edge budget multiplied by the number of required transitions, so a longer local deadline is not defeated by an unchanged total loop bound. The cap and retry count are configurable through `horizon_max_slow_time` and `horizon_max_retries`; the factor and padding use `escape_horizon_factor` and `escape_horizon_padding`. A cap smaller than the initial theory-based allowance raises a clear configuration error rather than silently shortening the requested run.

New output tables `correlation_phase_plane_events.csv` and `correlation_phase_plane_attempts.csv` retain final-run events and the replay history. Trial rows include initial/reference escape estimates, final per-edge and total budgets, retry count, elapsed time, unfinished-edge escape time and right-censoring status. Summaries retain a `timeout_fraction`; reliability cells with unfinished trials receive dotted hatching. The probability still uses the existing successful-cycle-plus-finite-escape criterion, and unresolved trials remain in its denominator.

The shared event simulator adds diagnostics only; its state updates and detection criteria are unchanged. The adaptive policy is applied only to the correlation phase-plane runner, not silently to the other experiments.

## Noise convention

`run_model_comparison.py` now reads per-case `fast_noise` and `noise_convention`. The orthogonal two-timescale case uses `fast_noise: 0.0001` and `noise_convention: per_step`, matching the repository's existing default noise convention. At each update, independent Gaussian increments of standard deviation 1e-4 are added to each fast-state component. Saliency remains unforced. The other two comparison cases receive zero additional noise. Fixed seeds reproduce identical noisy runs.

A `diffusion` option is also supported explicitly; it multiplies sigma by sqrt(dt_physical/tau_fast). It is not enabled in the supplied configurations. The output records the noise value and convention, avoiding ambiguity between per-step noise and an SDE diffusion coefficient.
